## 9. Compliance Checklist for All Funnel Copy
- Every email with returns or projections carries: "Projections are estimates, not guarantees. Results vary."
- No steering language (e.g., "great for families," "safe neighborhood"). Describe properties and data, not people.
- Equal Housing Opportunity logo/line in all email footers.
- CAN-SPAM: physical mailing address and one-click unsubscribe in every email.
- Tax and 1031 content always paired with "consult your CPA / Qualified Intermediary."
- Collect SSN/EIN and bank details only via secure, encrypted links.
