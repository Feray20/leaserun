Refund Policy

LeaseRun is digital software that you can use right after purchase. US law does not give a general right to return digital purchases, but we want you to be happy with what you pay for. This is our own policy:

  • Lender Package ($39, one-time): full refund within 30 days of purchase if you are not satisfied, no questions asked. Our lender-format guarantee also applies: if your lender rejects the format of the package, tell us within 60 days with the lender’s name and we refund the full price. The guarantee covers the format, not the lender’s credit decision.
  • Buyer Pass ($49 for 90 days): full refund within 14 days of purchase. After that, we refund the unused share of the 90 days on request if something did not work as described.
  • Investor Pro ($29 per month): cancel anytime online at leaserun.com/cancel – no further charges, and you keep access until the end of the paid month. If you forgot to cancel and have not used Investor Pro since the last renewal, we refund that month on request within 14 days.
  • Business plans: refunds as agreed in your business terms.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually post them within 5–10 business days. Questions: support@leaserun.com.
